# Bus vs mesh at 4, 9 and 16 cores (make mesh-scaling), model of the commit that added this file.
# The SoC's NoC is 2x2, so larger systems are a calibrated model (verif/scaling/tb_mesh_scaling.v):
# calibrated on results/2026-11-13/traffic.txt (bus and noc4_f256, private lines, 4 cores). The bus model
# is the same for every pattern, and keeps a 1-cycle bus at 9 and 16 cores (optimistic for the bus).

Bus vs mesh as the core count grows (model, verif/scaling/tb_mesh_scaling.v, 400 misses per core).
Each cell: misses per 1000 cycles (all cores) / average miss latency in cycles. gap = mean idle
cycles between a core's misses (smaller = more load). mesh/bus = mesh throughput over bus throughput.
